I am a PhD candidate in the School of Computing at the Australian National University.

My research is broadly concerned with formal verification and security. I am particularly interested in symbolic models of security protocols, knowledge problems such as intruder deduction and static equivalence, and their connections with term rewriting, word problems, automata, and algebraic structures.

More generally, I am interested in how structural properties of computational models can be used to understand decidability, complexity, and verification problems.
