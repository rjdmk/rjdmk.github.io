PhD Researcher · Formal Methods

Raja Damanik

I am a PhD researcher at the Australian National University. My research concerns formal methods for security protocol verification, particularly knowledge problems, term rewriting systems, and word rewriting systems.

Publications Download CV

Research

My research studies how algebraic and rewriting structures affect the decidability of knowledge problems arising in symbolic cryptographic analysis.

Security Protocol Verification

Symbolic models of attacker knowledge, including deduction and static equivalence.

Term Rewriting

Structural classes of rewrite systems and their connections with decidability and computational complexity.

String and Word Rewriting

Semi-Thue systems, divisibility problems, erasing rules, monoids, and Cayley graphs.
