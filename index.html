I am a PhD candidate in the School of Computing at the Australian National University.

My research is broadly concerned with formal verification and security. I am particularly interested in symbolic models of security protocols, knowledge problems such as intruder deduction and static equivalence, and their connections with term rewriting, word problems, automata, algebra, and logic.

More generally, I am interested in how structural properties of computational models can be used to understand decidability and verification problems.

Research

Formal security. Symbolic verification of cryptographic protocols, intruder knowledge, deduction, and static equivalence.
Rewriting and algebra. Term and string rewriting systems, word problems, Cayley graphs, and structural approaches to decidability.
Logic and verification. Logical and algorithmic methods for reasoning about computational systems.
